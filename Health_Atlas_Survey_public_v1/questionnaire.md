# Patient cost survey instrument (blank)

Student research on district-level out-of-pocket health spending in Bangladesh.

**Interviewer fields (not asked):** serial number · hospital · department · date · patient's sex · approximate age · interview completed (yes / no)

Administer in Bangla. Interview the person who paid for the care (the patient or the attendant). Target 10 to 12 minutes. Read the consent script, then ask the questions in order.

## Consent script (read aloud)

"My name is [interviewer]. I am a student doing an independent study on what healthcare costs families in Bangladesh. I would like to ask you about 20 short questions about the cost of the treatment you or your family member received here. It takes about ten minutes. I will not record your name or any information that identifies you, and nothing you say will affect your treatment at this hospital. You can stop at any time or skip any question. Is it okay to begin?"

## Section A. Screening and household

1. Are you the patient or an attendant? (patient / attendant: relation ____)
2. Is the patient 18 or older? (yes / no). If no, is the respondent the parent or guardian? (yes / no)
3. Which district does the patient's household live in? ____ (city / town / village)
4. How many people live in the household? ____
5. What is the main occupation of the household's main earner? ____
6. Roughly what does the household spend in a typical month, on everything? (under 15,000 / 15,000–30,000 / 30,000–60,000 / 60,000–100,000 / above 100,000 BDT)

## Section B. The illness episode

7. What is the patient being treated for? (infection / injury / heart / blood pressure / diabetes / maternal / cancer / surgery / other: ____)
8. Is this an outpatient visit or an admission? If admitted, how many nights? ____
9. When did the illness start? (____ days / ____ weeks ago)

## Section C. The care-seeking path

10. Where did you go FIRST when the illness started? (pharmacy or drug seller / village doctor or informal provider / community clinic / government hospital / private clinic or doctor / this hospital directly / did nothing at first)
11. How many different providers did you see before coming here? ____
12. Why did you come to this hospital? (referred / reputation / closest / cost / a doctor you know / other)
13. Did you take any medicine for this illness before a doctor prescribed it? (yes / no). If yes, what kind? (antibiotic / painkiller / other / do not know)

## Section D. Costs for this episode (BDT, including care before this hospital)

14. Doctors' and consultation fees, in total ____
15. Diagnostic tests and imaging ____
16. Medicines ____
17. Bed, procedure or operation charges ____
18. Transport to and from care, all trips ____
19. Food and lodging for attendants ____
20. Any other payments you were asked to make that were not on a bill (tips / payments to staff / brokers) ____
21. Did any insurance, employer, NGO or government scheme pay any part? (yes / no). How much? ____

## Section E. Financing and consequences

22. How did you pay? (savings / borrowed from relatives or friends / borrowed from a lender or NGO / sold or pawned an asset / help from relatives abroad / other: ____; more than one allowed)
23. How many days of work did the patient and attendants lose because of this illness? ____
24. Did cost cause you to do any of the following: delay coming to hospital / buy fewer medicines than prescribed / skip a test / leave earlier than advised?
25. Next time someone in the family is ill, where would you go first, and why? ____

## Changes recommended after waves 1–2

- Write the interview date on every form.
- Add: "Months since first care for this illness: ____".
- For any rate (per day / week / month), write the rate × the number of days / weeks / months.
- For dialysis: sessions per week × price per session × weeks.
- Keep a tally of people approached, refused and completed for every batch.
